How it works

Three moves.
That's the whole interface.

No menus to learn, no keyboard, no reading. A four-year-old can run the entire thing with a crayon, a voice and one finger.

01

Draw a character

Any creature counts. A blob with legs, a dinosaur in a hat, a very round cat. Hold it up to the camera and StorySpark meets them.

Crayon · paper · 20 seconds
02

Say what happens

“They're scared of the dark!” “She wants a sandwich!” Kids talk, StorySpark listens, and the story starts moving in their direction.

Talk or tap · no typing
03

Choose the next move

Two clear choices, read aloud. Whatever they pick, the lesson comes along for the ride — and the next chapter remembers it.

Listen · choose · keep going

For the grown-ups

Small humans.
Big guardrails.

StorySpark is built to be handed over — not hovered over. Every chapter is checked before your kid hears a word, and every session has a natural end.

Read the grown-ups guide →

Safety before story

Every chapter passes a child-safety check for kindness, age-fit and scare-factor before it plays. No cliffhangers designed to keep kids hooked.

Voice first, reading optional

Pre-readers can play the whole adventure by talking and tapping pictures. Text is there for the grown-ups who want it.

A real ending

Adventures run 7–20 minutes and finish with a calm “the end”, a printable drawing and one question to talk about.

Private by default

Drawings and recordings belong to your family. No profiles for kids, no ads, no feed, and nothing to scroll.

Ready when the crayons are

Tonight's story starts with a crayon.

Grab paper, draw a weird little creature, and let them decide what happens next. You bring the fridge door; we'll bring the adventure.
