Pack nothing. Bring your kindest thinking. The path starts at the yellow gate and ends under the paper stars.

What the quest practises

Kindness is a
set of small moves.

1

Notice

Spot the small signal that someone needs a hand — a dropped box, a quiet voice, a stuck gate.

2

Offer

Choose a concrete, manageable action. Not “be nice”, but “carry one box” or “save a seat”.

3

Reflect

At the end, name what happened in plain words. No score to beat, no sibling to outrun.
